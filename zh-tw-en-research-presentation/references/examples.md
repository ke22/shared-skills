# Calibration Examples

## 1. Clarifying questions

Source:
`追問對話設計促進反思及情境敏銳度`

Avoid:
`Probing design improves contextual sensitivity.`

Why:
- `probing design` is not the preferred mechanism label
- `improves` may overstate causality
- `contextual sensitivity` sounds like a pre-existing trait/construct

Preferred:
`Clarifying questions encourage reflection and awareness of contextual cues.`

If the project has defined its own construct for this outcome, use the label from the private registry (`references/private/project-constructs.md`), for example:
`Clarifying questions are designed to strengthen <project construct>.`

Only use that version when the construct is defined in the registry.

---

## 2. Empathy / validation

Source:
`附和同理`

Avoid:
`Agreeing and empathising`

Reason:
The Chinese label collapses several distinct interaction strategies.

Possible translations by actual behavior:

- feelings acknowledged → `empathic acknowledgement`
- emotion recognised → `empathic response`
- subjective experience accepted → `validation / validating communication`
- safety provided → `reassurance`
- argument avoided → `avoiding correction and argument`

Never translate validation as factual agreement with a hallucination or delusion.

---

## 3. Actionable advice

Source:
`具體執行建議`

Avoid:
`easy-to-execute suggestions`

Preferred:
`actionable guidance`

Alternative:
`specific and actionable recommendations`

## 4. Hallucination risk

Source:
`AI 幻覺防範`

Avoid:
`hallucination prevention`

Preferred:
`hallucination mitigation`

Reason:
`prevention` may imply a guarantee that hallucination will not occur.

---

## 5. Field pilot

Source:
`原型已經過一輪專家評測，並經修正後投入場域中進行 Pilot 實驗。`

Preferred:
`The prototype underwent one round of expert evaluation and was revised before a field pilot.`

Avoid:
`The prototype was clinically validated before deployment.`

Reason:
`clinical validation` overstates the evidence and changes the evaluation type.

---

## 6. Quantitative result

Source:
`照顧服務信心度從 4.20 提升至 4.80`（illustrative values）

Preferred:
`Mean care confidence increased from 4.20 to 4.80.`

Avoid:
`The intervention significantly improved carers' self-efficacy.`

Reason:
- confidence is not automatically self-efficacy
- statistical significance is not provided

---

## 7. Taiwan LTC role

Source:
`居服員`

Preferred first use when audience needs context:
`home care worker (a formal frontline care role in Taiwan's LTC system)`

Subsequent use:
`home care worker`

Avoid:
- `caregiver` when this would be confused with family carer
- substituting a specific Australian occupation without evidence of equivalence

## 8. Research-defined construct

Hypothetical example (real project constructs are in the private registry):

Source:
`照護訊息落差`（defined by the project as: carers receive guidance after the moment they needed it）

Preferred project label (from the registry):
`guidance-timing gap`

First introduction:
`a guidance-timing gap, where guidance reaches the carer only after the moment it was needed`

Avoid:
presenting the label as an established construct, or equating it with a related published one (e.g. the KTA `know-do gap`).

Reason:
A project concept may be theoretically related to a published construct but is not automatically identical. If the term has no registry entry, flag `RESEARCH_CONSTRUCT_UNDEFINED`.

## 10. Controlled reformulation

Source:
`AI Chatbot 透過追問設計、附和同理、易執行建議，打破使用者和科技間的冰冷感，提升對話詢問體驗及支持感。`

Literal-but-poor:
`Through clarification design, empathetic agreement, and easy-to-execute suggestions, the chatbot breaks the cold feeling between users and technology.`

Presentation version:
`The chatbot combines clarifying questions, empathic responses, and actionable guidance to create a more supportive care experience.`

Tag internally:
`CONTROLLED_REFORMULATION`

Before accepting, verify that the source actually supports each of:
- clarification
- empathy
- actionable guidance
- support experience
