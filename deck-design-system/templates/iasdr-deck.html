How should we

Typesetting

Two languages share one grid, not one type scale

Chinese glyphs fill the full em square

(Design note)

At the same pixel size, Chinese looks larger and denser than English, so Chinese titles are set about 10% smaller with more line height.

English titles tighten; Chinese titles never do

(Design note)

English display and title text use slight negative tracking. Chinese keeps zero tracking, because narrower spacing makes characters collide.

Cross, N. (1999). Natural intelligence in design. Design Studies, 20(1), 25–39.

Line length

Readable lines are counted differently in each language

English body reads best at 60–75 characters per line; Chinese at 22–32 characters.

English

Count characters, including spaces. Break at words, and never hyphenate a title.

Chinese

Count full-width characters. Keep closing punctuation off the start of a line.

排版原則Typesetting

兩種語言共用格線，但不共用字級

中文字面占滿整個字身框

（設計說明）

同樣的像素大小，中文看起來比英文更大、更密，所以中文標題縮小約一成，並加大行距。

英文標題收緊字距，中文標題不收

（設計說明）

英文大標與標題使用略為負的字距；中文維持 0，字距收窄會讓字與字黏在一起。

Cross, N. (1999). Natural intelligence in design. Design Studies, 20(1), 25–39.

行長Line length

兩種語言的易讀行長要分開計算

英文內文每行 60–75 個字元最好讀；中文每行 22–32 個字。

英文

以字元計，含空白。依單字斷行，標題不使用連字號。

中文

以全形字計。句末標點不放在行首。
